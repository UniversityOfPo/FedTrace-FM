## 3. IEEE multiple-submission and prior-publication policy (VERIFIED, IEEE conference author center)

- "multiple submission" = "a given article being concurrently under active consideration by two or more
  publications"; authors "must disclose all prior publication(s) and current submissions".
- Staged publication (workshop → conference → journal) is recognised but "must be fully referenced"; recycled text
  / figures must be indicated and the new submission must state how it differs.
- Source: https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-policies/
- ICASSP 2027's own dual-submission wording was not found (UNRESOLVED).
