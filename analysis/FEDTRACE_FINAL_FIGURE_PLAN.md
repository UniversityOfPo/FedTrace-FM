## 3. Per-Figure Design Notes

**Figure 1 (conceptual):** deliberately contains zero experimental numbers — a pure pipeline diagram. Explicitly labels the two structurally different calibration windows (cross-client round reference `c_r` vs. per-client longitudinal calibration) as a dedicated annotation, per the task's Part 2 instruction, without cluttering the boxes themselves. Shows `τ=3`/`P=3` once, at the decision-rule box, not repeated elsewhere.

**Figure 2 (`EXP_018` onset trajectory):** three stacked panels (`z^m`, `z^t_res`, `S`), window **rounds 15–30**, chosen deliberately to **exclude round 0** — the known self-inclusive calibration artifact (`FEDTRACE_MASTER_RESEARCH_STATUS.md` §7) is outside this window by construction, and the window is stated explicitly in the title/caption rather than silently cropped (Option (a) from the task's Part 3 instruction; the artifact itself is not hidden — it is disclosed in the caption text, §7 below, rather than plotted). The malicious client (`client_002`, bold red) is visually distinct from the honest envelope (thin gray lines) in every panel. Threshold, true onset (`r=20`), and confirmation (`r=22`) are all marked. The `z^t_res` panel makes the "reacts sharply exactly at onset" claim visually unambiguous; the `z^m` panel makes the "stays near the honest envelope" claim equally unambiguous — together this is the figure's core evidentiary content.

**Figure 3 (signal complementarity):** 2×2, rows = attack family, columns = signal (`z^m` / `z^t_res`), both seeds overlaid per cell (solid = seed 123, dashed = seed 2026) for the malicious client only. Visually demonstrates: Gaussian row → `z^m` panel shows a large, immediate jump; `z^t_res` panel shows a smaller/mixed response. Directional row → the reverse. **Caption language is restricted to "attack-family coverage / complementary evidence," never "fusion beats the best single detector"** — matching `analysis/FEDTRACE_MASTER_RESEARCH_STATUS.md` §4/C5's already-established, evidence-checked constraint (the combined score never outperformed its best single component in any of the 8 attack runs on record).

**Figure 4 (Multi-Krum geometry dichotomy):** left panel is a 2×2 grayscale-safe matrix (black cells for 100%, white for 0%, with the exact percentage always printed inside the cell — never relying on color alone) showing the fully-crossed, zero-exception empirical dichotomy; right panel is a representative (seed 123) selected/rejected step-timeline for both attack families side by side, making the "instant regime switch" vs. "no switch at all" contrast directly visible. Caption states explicitly this is an empirical result under the tested configuration (`n=5`, `f=1`, `α=0.1`, `ρ=1.0`/`c=0`), not a universal theorem.

**Figure 5 (aggregation ≠ attribution):** a minimal two-row timeline. FedTrace v3 correctly confirms `client_002` at `r=22`; the Multi-Krum full-run proxy confirms the honest `client_004` at `r=4` — **before the attack even begins** — and the figure explicitly annotates that the true attacker is never rejected at any post-onset round. This is the figure's single message, delivered with minimal chart junk.

**Figure 6 (appendix — utility 2×2):** two small multi-panels (Gaussian / Directional), each a grouped-bar FedAvg-vs-Multi-Krum comparison across both seeds, clean and attacked. Deliberately restricted to Top-1 only (no Top-5 clutter, per the task's "avoid stuffing many metrics" instruction). Caption states "not a significance claim" directly in the title, not only in body text.

**Figure 7 (appendix — partition fairness):** two per-seed bar charts of `client_partition_summary.csv` sample counts, with the persistently-rejected client (per `Research_Database/FedTrace_Master_Results.xlsx`'s Multi-Krum Diagnostics Summary sheet, computed in Phase P2) highlighted. Caption uses **"replicated association under the tested `α=0.1` configuration"** verbatim, and contains no causal language anywhere (title, axis labels, or annotations).

**Figure 8 (appendix — generalization check):** a compact timeline showing all three conditions (`EXP_018` anchor, `EXP_026` identity change, `EXP_027` onset change), each computed **live** from its own `client_round_trace.csv` via `src/analysis/fedtrace_detector.py::evaluate_detector_v3` (upgraded during this task from an initial hardcoded-literal version specifically to satisfy the "verify all plotted values before plotting" / "use raw artifacts, not prose" requirements — see §6). **Explicit recommendation, stated here and in the figure's own provenance row: this content is inherently tabular (7 short fields × 3 rows) and is very likely a stronger fit for a Phase P5 table than for this figure.** It is retained in the appendix figure set as a usable visual if a figure is preferred over a table in the final manuscript layout, but P5 should default to representing this content as a table unless there is a specific layout reason to keep a figure instead.

## 7. Defects Found and Fixed During This Task

- **Figure 1, first render:** an explanatory annotation text box overlapped and rendered on top of the "Magnitude branch" box, making both illegible. **Fixed** by enlarging the figure, reserving a dedicated top band for the annotation, and re-spacing every box downward — re-inspected and confirmed clean in the corrected render.
- **Figure 2, first render:** the `z^t_res` panel labels/titles rendered as `z_r^t es` (a malformed, broken subscript) because the underlying LaTeX used an unbraced `z^t_res` (mathtext parses `_r` as the subscript and leaves `es` as stray text). **Fixed** by bracing the subscript (`z^t_{\mathrm{res}}`) and using `\max` instead of plain `max` for the combined-score panel; re-inspected and confirmed correct in the corrected render.
- **Figure 8:** upgraded from a hardcoded-literal data table to a live computation via the frozen detector (§3/§6), specifically to close a "prose-document-sourced" provenance gap the task's Part 1/15 instructions flagged as lower-priority than raw-artifact sourcing.

No other defect was found on visual inspection of the corrected renders.
